Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about services, credits, orders, and service codes.

What does a service code (e.g. BLEN-1042) on the dashboard mean?

Every service order has an identifier in the form CODE-####. The letters indicate the service type (see the Service code legend table beside it), and the number increments in order of creation. This code appears under Reports (Kanban & List) and is used when opening a support ticket.

How long until a backlink gets indexed?

The Indexer (IDX) pipeline automatically submits, checks, and retries. Typically 70–90% of links are recognized within 1–7 days; harder links keep being retried until they're indexed.

How do I top up & use credits?

Top up credits under Credit & Invoices (or the “Top up” button in the sidebar). When you order a service, the system deducts credits for the selected plan and shows your remaining balance before you confirm.

Can I buy a single service on its own?

Yes. You can buy each service separately (CT, BLEN, IDX…) or combine them into a bundle. Some services, like Keyword Research (KW), are included free when you buy Content or Backlinks.

Refund / cancellation policy?

Orders in the Planned (not started) status can be canceled and refunded in credits. Orders that have moved to In progress are billed for the work already completed.

How do I reach an advisor?

Go to the Support page to chat live, call the hotline, or submit a ticket. Response targets: chat < 5 minutes, urgent tickets < 2 hours.
