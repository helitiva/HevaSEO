New websites often lack the data Google and AI systems need to understand **who you are**, **what area you serve**, and **what expertise you have**. Entity SEO solves this foundation by unifying your brand information across many trusted touchpoints — from business profiles and social profiles to citations and content that clearly explains your field.

## Why entities matter

Search engines no longer just match keywords. They build a knowledge graph of *entities* — people, brands, places, and topics — and the relationships between them. When your brand is a well-defined entity with consistent signals, you become eligible for richer results and AI citations.

- **Consistent NAP** (name, address, phone) across directories
- **Authoritative citations** from sources Google already trusts
- **Clear topical content** that explains exactly what you do

## How it fits with content and indexing

When entity signals are combined with articles that answer your customers' questions, and backlinks that actually get indexed, your website has far more signals to be judged trustworthy. That's why HevaSEO runs **content, entity, and indexer** as one system instead of disconnected pieces.

> A strong entity is the difference between "a website that exists" and "a brand Google and AI can confidently recommend."
